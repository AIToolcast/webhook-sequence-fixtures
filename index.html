    Choose the real contract

    These are generic fictional event objects. They do not implement Stripe, Shopify or n8n payloads, signatures or ordering rules. Similar events do not automatically belong to the same business operation.

    The changed-content case deliberately contradicts an immutable-event contract. It is not normal Stripe retry behavior.

    Run only in an isolated test

    The bundle sends no requests and reports no pass/fail result. Its overlapping-delivery case is a plan for an external runner; a JSON array does not create concurrency or prove atomicity.

    The tool does not reset state. Inspect actual destination outcomes and failure recovery separately. Do not send these examples to production.

    Method, privacy and reproducibility

    The generator uses fixed fictional templates and two short labels in your browser. It sends no fixture data, stores no input and executes no workflow. Hosting requests and links you open are separate. Generated files include your labels. Read the site privacy notice.
